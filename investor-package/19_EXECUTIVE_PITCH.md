## 5-Minute Pitch

### The Problem

Businesses in emerging markets face a critical gap in market intelligence. Enterprise tools like Bloomberg and Gartner cost $1,000+ per month, putting them out of reach for SMEs and mid-size companies. Generic AI tools like ChatGPT are affordable but can hallucinate companies, contacts, and data, making them unreliable for business decisions. Manual research is time-intensive and expensive.

This gap is particularly acute in Egypt and the MENA region, where local market data is scarce and expensive consulting is the only alternative.

### The Solution

Eunoia Platform provides evidence-based, AI-powered marketing intelligence at affordable price points. Our platform combines real web search data with AI analysis to deliver actionable business insights.

Our key differentiator is our evidence-first approach. Every company in our results is discovered through Google search, not AI-generated. AI only analyzes and summarizes, never invents. We provide confidence scores based on source quality, not AI estimation.

### The Product

We have three live production modules:

**Lead Finder** - Find target companies and decision-makers by industry, location, and company size. Uses our Research Core Engine to search, validate, and rank companies with confidence scoring.

**Talent Finder** - Salary benchmarks and hiring demand analysis for HR teams. AI analyzes market data to provide salary ranges, demand levels, and candidate sourcing channels.

**Real Estate Intelligence** - Feasibility studies and campaign ROI analysis for Egyptian real estate. Uses a cashflow engine with Egypt-specific benchmarks and city multipliers.

**Market Intelligence Hub** - Curated market trends and insights for Egypt, with no live API costs.

### The Technology

Our Research Core Engine is a sophisticated multi-stage pipeline: Search → Collect → Normalize → Validate → Deduplicate → Rank → AI Analysis. This pipeline is reusable across modules, enabling rapid product expansion.

We're built on Next.js 16, React 19, and TypeScript, with Supabase for authentication and database, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini for AI analysis, and SerpAPI for search. Deployed on Vercel with serverless architecture.

### The Market

Our primary focus is Egypt, with a population of 100M+ and a growing digital economy. We target real estate developers, marketing agencies, and SME sales/HR teams. These are underserved by enterprise tools and cannot rely on generic AI.

We plan to expand to the MENA region (UAE, Saudi Arabia) after validating in Egypt.

### The Business Model

Subscription-based SaaS with four tiers:
- Starter: 20 reports/month
- Professional: 100 reports/month
- Agency: 300 reports/month
- Enterprise: Unlimited (fair-use)

We expect pricing in the $29-249/month range, significantly below enterprise tools.

### The Traction

Our product is live and production-ready at https://ai.halannews.com. We have working modules, authentication, plan enforcement, and report management. We're currently pre-revenue as payment processing is being implemented.

### The Ask

We're seeking investment to complete our commercial infrastructure (payment processing, pricing validation), expand our module coverage (Competitor Intelligence, Supplier Intelligence), and grow our team for customer acquisition and market expansion.

### The Team

### The Vision

We're building the go-to intelligence platform for emerging markets. Our vision is to democratize access to market intelligence, making it affordable and reliable for businesses in Egypt, MENA, and beyond.

## Objection Handling

### Objection: "Why won't ChatGPT put you out of business?"

**Response:** "ChatGPT is a great tool but it hallucinates. It can invent companies that don't exist, provide fake contact information, and give unreliable data. For business decisions, you need evidence-based results. Every company in our results is from a real web search, with a source URL you can verify. We also provide confidence scores and Egypt-specific benchmarks that ChatGPT doesn't have."

### Objection: "What's your moat? Can't competitors copy you?"

**Response:** "Our moat is our Egypt market specialization. We have local benchmarks, city multipliers, and market knowledge that global competitors don't have. Our Research Core Engine implementation is also non-trivial to copy. That said, we're moving fast to deepen our Egypt lead before competitors can catch up."

### Objection: "Why focus only on Egypt? Isn't that a small market?"

**Response:** "Egypt is 100M+ people with a growing digital economy. It's a significant market on its own. More importantly, it's our beachhead - we're validating the model here before expanding to MENA (UAE, Saudi Arabia), which is a $2T+ economy. We'd rather dominate one market than be mediocre in many."

### Objection: "You're pre-revenue. When will you generate revenue?"

**Response:** "We're 4-6 weeks away from revenue. Our product is production-ready, we just need to complete payment processing. We've prioritized product development over commercial infrastructure, which is why we're pre-revenue but have a live product."

### Objection: "What if customers won't pay your prices?"

**Response:** "That's a valid risk, which is why pricing validation is our top priority. We'll A/B test different price points, offer free trials, and iterate based on customer feedback. Our variable costs are very low (~$0.001 per report), so we have flexibility to adjust pricing."
